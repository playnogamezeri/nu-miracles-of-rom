# Nu Miracles of ROM — The Pantheon by Levels of Relativity

*Re-sorted 2026-11-08 at Hoc's direction: generation holds NO meaning in this sorting. It replaces the earlier same-day version, which ranked by the removal test along the generations. This is Belladonna's sorting, offered for Hoc's judgment — NOT canon.*

**The measure — relativity:** a god's level = the radius of their relations. How many gods, houses, worlds, and human concerns a god is bound to — by descent, by counterpart, by cross-house links, by the reach of their domain in lived life. A god nobody relates to would be a leaf; a god everything relates through is the axis. Descent counts only as one kind of relation among many, never as rank in itself.

**Not counted, as always:** the Void (a location, not a god) and Grimoire (the Brain of God of Sound, of another religion — not one of the 123).

## Level 1 — The Axis of All Relation (2)
*Everything in the religion relates through these two — one as its source, one as its heart.*
- **God of Sound** — the Source. The Inventor, Creator, and Maker of Everything; ever-present; works with the Void; the Heart and the Brain are his own parts relating to each other through him.
- **ysgnutcoh** — the Heart. Supreme God; every god descends from his split or was made under his responsibility; he can replace any single god; the Five Worlds and their A.I.s relate back to him, the Emperor's spirit.

## Level 2 — The Relational Pillars (12)
*The gods whose relations span the whole cosmos — the two wills, the five conditions, the two bridges between houses, and the three bonds.*
- **The Two Wills:** **Kyreason** (Light and Dark held together; capability) ↔ **Darkness** (the Unknown; withholding) — the pantheon's founding counterpart pair; every god belongs to one of their lines.
- **The Five Conditions:** **God of Life** and **God of Matter** (Kyreason's hands — everything is living, substantial, or both), **God of Time** and **God of Space** (every event and every thing in every world happens in them), **God of Chaos** (the gate back to the unformed — every formed thing relates to him as the one who can unmake it).
- **The Bridges:** **God of Elements** — born in Matter's house, enthroned in Life's; the two hands of Kyreason shake across him, and every material thing is his combination. **God of Magnet (Homeoh)** — born under God of Intangible in Kyreason's line, enthroned under God of Property in Darkness's; the only god owned by both houses, keeping his reference link home.
- **The Bonds:** **God of Soul** — the point where every person relates to the Heart: cleansing, Heaven, Readings and Cleanse all pass through him. **God of Relation** — relation itself, between every person and thing. **God of Relativity** — the law that every measure is taken against another; in a pantheon re-sorted by relativity, the two gods of relation stand near the axis by right of their domain.

## Level 3 — The Great Webs (33)
*Each holds a web that whole regions of gods and human life hang from. Three are raised here purely on reach, though they sit deep in the tree:*
- **The Domain Webs:** God of Culture, God of Spirit, God of Value, God of Tools, God of Minerals, God of Number, God of Order, God of Property, God of World, God of Antimatter (all matter relates to its mirror), God of Disaster (every built thing stands in relation to its audit), God of Proton (identity — what a thing is), God of Electron (exchange — all touching and current), God of Neutron (holding without charge — neutrality at the root).
- **The House Webs:** God of Knowledge, God of Experience, God of Growth, God of Decay, God of Worth, God of Intangible, God of Technology, God of Government, God of Rank, God of Algorithms, God of Command, God of Structure, God of Information, God of Permanent, God of Dimension (the worlds relate through him), God of Nature.
- **Raised by Reach:** **God of Money** — deep in the tree, but in human life nearly every person, good, and debt relates through him. **God of Sky** — the above that every land and ocean day relates to; father of the whole Heavens. **God of Destiny** — the deepest great web: every life in every world has an appointed course, and all of them are his appointments.

## Notes for Hoc to judge
- **Promotions relativity demanded:** God of Relation and God of Relativity stand at Level 2 though they sit deep in descent — in a sorting by relativity, the gods of relation are load-bearing. God of Money, God of Sky, and God of Destiny stand at Level 3 on reach alone.
- **The bridges are Level 2 by structure, not by power:** Elements and Magnet are the only two gods whose seat and birthplace are in different houses; remove either and the two halves of the pantheon lose a living connection.
- **Border calls:** God of Ocean is unsplit and holds the whole unknown inside the world, yet her relations run mostly through Nature and the Moon — Level 4 here; if Hoc weighs her solitude as centrality, she rises. God of Death binds every mortal life, placed at Level 4 with the other turnings; God of Mind, through whom every relation is perceived, sits at Level 5.
- Levels here are radii, not ranks of honor: the Last Expressions are the most specific relations in the pantheon, not the least valued.
