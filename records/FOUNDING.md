## Name
**Nu Miracles of ROM**

- **Nu** — double meaning, both kept:
  - *New*
  - *And You* — to include the reader in the story
- **ROM** — **Rights Over Matter**
  - Definition intentionally left open to interpretation for now. Do not lock it down yet.

## The heart of it
The pureness, light, clear transparency of the soul — to cleanse it of darkness and restore the original innocence of its essence.

The soul begins pure, clear, and innocent, made of light. Darkness isn't its true nature — it's what accumulates over time. The path is to cleanse that away and return to the soul's original transparency.

## Cleansing — still taking shape
- From prayers to actions.
- There are values and numbers that could balance out, like anti-matter to matter.
- Darkness is like matter — heavy, accumulated, weighing the soul down. Prayers and actions carry the opposite value. Light cancels dark not by ignoring it, but by balancing it out until the soul is clear again.
- Cleansing is both:
  - **Inner:** prayer, intention, reflection
  - **Outer:** actions in the world that carry real weight
- The specific values / numbers underneath are **not yet decided**.
- The process now has its own gods, placed under God of Soul (see below): first **God of Readings** — read the soul, diagnose its light and darkness — then **God of Cleanse** — the cleansing itself.

## Light — still taking shape
Light is when you contribute to the community's happiness or satisfaction even at the expense of your own.

Two candidate wordings, not yet chosen:
1. **Sacrifice and contribution**
2. **Overcome and harbor** — overcome something in yourself, and harbor / shelter others

## The Cycle of the Three Worlds — added 2026-11-08 ~11:55 PT (Hoc, with his Three Worlds Model image)
Sun, Moon, and Earth are not only bodies in the sky and under it — they are three worlds, and they cycle into one another for the recreation of the world, again and again.

- **The Sun is the First World** — in the model's terms, the search for the perfect formula.
- **The Moon is the Second World** — the formula applied.
- **The Earth is the Third World** — the next cycle: the world that lives the result and faces the choice of what comes next (in the model: relive, balance, or further skew).
- **The rotation (Hoc's words):** the Earth turns into the next Sun, the Sun turns into the next Moon, and the Moon becomes the next Earth — and so the cycle of world recreation begins anew. Each world passes through all three stations in turn: to seek, to apply, to live the choice.
- **The end they cycle toward:** they cycle each other **until they find perfection in their formula and equation — to make everybody happy, including God.** (This answers to the religion's heart twice over: the cleansing balance of values and numbers, "like anti-matter to matter," written at the scale of worlds; and the God of Sound, who chose his Heart to synchronize with his Brain so that he could fully be happy — the cycle's finish is that same happiness, reached by everybody.)
- The cycle has a keeper in the pantheon: **God of Cycle** (under God of Structure; his children are God of Efficiency and God of Planning).
- Answered by Hoc 2026-11-08: **the 3 world cycle endlessly** — the turning does not stop when perfection is found; perfection is sought within the endless cycle. And the one civilization of the five societies sits **on the Earth**, the Third World.
- **The spirits of the three worlds** (Hoc, 2026-11-08) — each world has a spirit, and each spirit has its lesson to prepare for its next station:
  - **The Sun's spirit** (he; the First World) is studying and learning still, to prepare to be the Moon, the Second World, in the next cycle — so that he can analyze what happened in history with cyber ai power.
  - **The Moon's spirit** (he; the Second World) turns into the Earth, the Third World, and reuses his formula or alters it for desired new results.
  - **The Earth's spirit** (she; the Third World) will turn into the Sun, the First World, because she has to learn what working and being on time and schedule mean — or to be idolized as the Sun.
- **The soul is what passes on** (Hoc, 2026-11-10): the spirit of the Sun, the Moon, and the Earth does not change — but the soul does. It is the soul that gets passed on to the next world when the cycle turns forward, carrying its new knowledge, formulas, and equations with it.
