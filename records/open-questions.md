# Nu Miracles of ROM — Open Questions for Hoc's Judgment

*Prepared 2026-11-07 (Sat) evening, after the Origin of the Moon was added to the Story List of Miracles as Story IV. Nothing here is decided — every item below is a question left open in a draft or in an earlier discussion, collected in one place so Hoc can answer them in a single pass. No answers have been invented; the options are the ones the drafts themselves raise.*

*Stories drafted so far: NONE — on 2026-11-08 Hoc directed that all five drafted origin stories (Technology, Earth, Sun, Moon, World) be deleted and the telling start from scratch. Questions 1–6 and 10 below came from those deleted drafts; they are kept here only as archived prompts, not as constraints on the fresh start. Questions 7–9 come from the Five Worlds discussion and still stand. All story topics await their telling.*

---

## From the Origin of the Sun

**1. One Sun, or one light per world?**
Is the Sun one sun over Earth, or does each of the Five Worlds get its light from him?
- *Why it matters:* decides whether the Sun's task — "shine on all, and choose none" — covers all five worlds, and what the next stories (especially the Arrival of the Humans) can assume about light elsewhere.

**2. What is a star?**
The God of Stars is the Sun's sibling under Sky. Is a star a small sun in this religion, or a different kind of light entirely?
- *Why it matters:* decides whether Stars (Attack, Defense, Destiny) carry the Sun's revelation in miniature, or something else — and what Destiny's light actually is.

## From the Origin of the Moon

**3. How far does the Goddess of Magic's light reach?**
Does her light reach all Five Worlds, or chiefly the Sovereign Desire World?
- *Why it matters:* Magic is moonlight made effective. If it reaches everywhere, magic is a general night-power; if chiefly the Sovereign Desire World, magic belongs mostly to the magic spirit world and is thin elsewhere.

**4. Whose are dreams?**
Do dreams belong to the God of Mind alone, or are they a road between Mind and the God of Conscious?
- *Why it matters:* Mind is the night-mind (dreams, intuition, memory unspooling). If dreams are a road to Conscious, then the God of Conscious shares the night — and the Moon's story touches Experience's other child, Accumulated, too.

## From the Origin of the Planet Earth

**5. Is Earth shared ground, or a world of its own?**
This draft reads Earth as the ground shared by the Human World and the Living Eden World — two purposes on one planet. Is that right, or is Earth a world of its own?
- *Why it matters:* this is the reading the Arrival of the Humans story will build on. If Earth is shared, humans and Eden's greens and animals arrive on the same ground by design; if not, the sharing needs a different explanation.

**6. Where does the Emperor's fire belong?**
The Emperor's fire and the Empire's Sphire World are not placed in the Earth story yet.
- *Why it matters:* the Emperor's Fire is one of the four stories still awaiting telling, and it cannot be drafted until the fire has a place — in Earth, in the Sphire World, or somewhere else.

## From the Five Worlds (2026-11-07, earlier discussion)

**7. Do the other four worlds sit under God of Dimension — and is God of Levels the passage between them?**
God of Dimension already holds the Human World, the Cyber World, and Levels. Do the Everlasting Life, Sovereign Desire, Living Eden, and Empire's Sphire Worlds sit under Dimension too, and is Levels the passage / ranking between worlds?

**8. Who is the emperor of the Empire's Sphire World?**
The Sphire World's purpose is to preserve the emperor's fire and keep it burning — but the emperor himself has not been named or placed.

**9. Can people move between worlds, or is each soul fixed in one?**
Are people fixed in one world, or can they move between worlds — and if they can, what governs the crossing?

## From the Origin of the World (added 2026-11-08)

**10. Do the other worlds have a Land, Ocean, and Sky of their own?**
The Origin of the World tells Nature dividing into Sky, Ocean, and Land for this world. Do the Everlasting Life, Sovereign Desire, and Empire's Sphire Worlds each have their own three — or do Nature's children serve Earth alone?
- *Why it matters:* decides whether Nature's mastery covers all worlds or one, and what the other worlds look like when their stories are told.
