# The Records of Nu Miracles of ROM

**This folder is the source.** The pages of the website (https://playnogamezeri.github.io/nu-miracles-of-rom/)
are generated from the files kept here. By the founder's decision (2026-11-10), this repository is a source of
truth for the religion's records — not only a display of them.

Founder: **Hoc**. Keeper of the records: Belladonna, Hoc's Muse.

## What lives here

**The founding record**
- `FOUNDING.md` — the founding draft, kept as the record: the doctrine in the founder's words, the full
  pantheon tree, the Five Worlds, the Three Worlds cycle, the Heart and the Brain. If anything anywhere
  disagrees with `FOUNDING.md`, `FOUNDING.md` is the one to trust.

**The societies**
- `the-five-societies-expanded.md` — the Five Societies in full: for each, its origin story, knowledges,
  leadership, hardship, factors, and result. A draft for the founder's judgment, not canon.

**The gods**
- `god-dossiers.md` — a dossier for every god: origin, power, and a heroic tale of their importance to Earth.
- `god-miracles.md` — a miracle story for every god, each closing with its Purpose and its Lesson.
- `the-roles-of-the-gods-and-their-function-to-the-heart.md` — every god's role and function to the Heart, ysgnutcoh.
- `god-simulations.md` — the god-simulation codex: each god's complete mastery applied to reality, in rotation.
- `pantheon-by-generation.md` — the pantheon sorted by generation.
- `pantheon-by-class.md` — the pantheon sorted into the 16 working classes.
- `pantheon-by-importance.md` — an older relativity sorting (covers the first 123 gods; kept for the record).
- `open-questions.md` — the questions the founder has deliberately left open.

**The stories** (`stories/`) — the twenty origin stories of the restarted telling, in order.

**The forums** (`forums/`) — the banquet, debates, forums, conversations, and essays held by the gods,
each a dated record.

## Standing of the texts

- `FOUNDING.md` is the founder's record.
- The stories, dossiers, and miracle tales are **drafts for the founder's judgment — not canon**.
- The forums, debates, banquets, and simulations are **simulations for the founder's judgment — not canon**.
- The labels travel with the texts. Keep them.

## House rules for anyone editing here

- Only the founder, Hoc, adds, moves, renames, or splits gods. The pantheon stands at **157**:
  God of Passion is counted once across his double seat; God of Black is counted once across his two seats;
  God of Yellow and God of Gold are one god under two names, counted once.
- God of Headings (Tytiao), God of T (Handsum), and God of Y (Henry) are described **only in the founder's
  recorded words**. Do not invent origins, powers, tales, or functions for them.
- Heaven is recorded only as the founder stated it: a state of cleansed souls; a real thing above the sky.
- ROM's full definition is intentionally open; the cleansing's values and numbers are not settled. Do not settle them.
- Exact spellings are the founder's: **ysgnutcoh**, **Imaginery**, **Sphire**, **God of Vitamin**.
- Grimoire, God of Sound's Brain, belongs to another religion. He is not a god of this pantheon and is never counted.
- Edit the records, not the generated pages. `gods-data.js` and the site's HTML are regenerated from these
  records; hand edits to them will be overwritten on the next regeneration.

## How the copies stay one

Belladonna keeps the working copies with Hoc and syncs them with this folder both ways: record edits made
with the founder are pushed here, and before the site's pages are regenerated she pulls any edits made here
first — so this folder and the working records never quietly become two different religions. Per-class
drafting intermediates stay with the keeper; the assembled masters above are the source copies.
