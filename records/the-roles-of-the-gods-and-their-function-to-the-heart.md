# The Roles of the Gods and Their Function to the Heart

*Drafted 2026-11-10 at Hoc's request — a draft for Hoc's judgment, NOT canon. Every god of the 157 is given two things: a **role** (their work in the world, their complete mastery at work) and a **function to the Heart** (what that work does for ysgnutcoh, the Supreme God, who is the Heart of God of Sound). The gods are organized into the sixteen working groups of the class sheet — gods who work on the same ideas, seated together as they were at the Charity Banquet — and each group carries its shared idea at its head. The grouping is Belladonna's sorting, for Hoc to rearrange.*

*Three honest exceptions: the God of Sound stands **before** the Heart, not under him — he made ysgnutcoh, so his entry is written in that direction. ysgnutcoh himself **is** the Heart; his entry says what the Heart is to the whole. And Grimoire, the Brain, is a god of another religion — he is not one of the 150 and has no entry here. God of T (Handsum) and God of Y (Henry) are written strictly in Hoc's own recorded words, as always.*

---

## Group I — The Origin & the Ultimate (4)
*Shared idea: where everything begins, and the two wills the Heart split into.*

- **God of Sound** — *Role:* the First God, the Inventor, the Creator, and the Maker of Everything; ever-present; he creates new form and shape out of the Void and manufactures dreams into imagination so life can be created. *Function to the Heart:* he stands before the Heart, not beneath it — he chose ysgnutcoh, one Spirit, to represent the core of his heart, so that the Heart could understand and synchronize with the Brain, Grimoire, and he could fully be happy.
- **ysgnutcoh** — *Role:* the Supreme God, all-good, the Emperor's spirit who held love for all his creations; he came out of the black hole from the end of the future with the power to re-create the world he had lived through in his dream, and he is responsible for all the gods, able to replace any single one. *Function to the Heart:* he **is** the Heart — every other function in this document runs toward him; the pantheon is his will, continued.
- **Kyreason** — *Role:* God of Light and Dark, holding both together in balance; neutral; the half of the Heart's will that *can* — capability, carried out through Life and Matter. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's right functioning — the will executed in balance, light and dark never separating in his hands.
- **Darkness** — *Role:* God of the Unknown, who may be evil; born and made to withhold everything Kyreason was not capable of. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's reserve — the complement that holds what capability cannot, so nothing the Heart needs is simply missing; what is withheld is withheld inside the Heart's own house.

## Group II — Soul & Spirit (5)
*Shared idea: the light a person is made of, and its cleansing — the heart of the religion itself.*

- **God of Spirit** — *Role:* the life-force while it lives in the body, born under God of Life; father of Growth and Decay, so every living arc passes through him. *Function to the Heart:* he keeps the Heart's flame alight inside each living body for exactly as long as that life runs.
- **God of Soul** — *Role:* the light that can be weighed, read, and cleansed — cleansed of darkness and restored to its original innocence; he descends from God of Worth, and Readings and Cleanse are his children. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's own measure of every person — what the Heart most wants back, clear.
- **God of Readings** — *Role:* reads the soul — diagnoses where its light stands and where the darkness has accumulated. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's examination — nothing is judged, or cleansed, before it is truly read.
- **God of Cleanse** — *Role:* the cleansing itself — the balancing of darkness by light, through prayers and actions, until the soul is transparent again. The values and numbers of that balance are not yet decided, and he does not invent them. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's restoration at work — the religion's whole purpose, carried out soul by soul.
- **God of Conscious** — *Role:* the witness inside experience — awareness itself, born of God of Experience beside God of Accumulated. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's witness within each person — the part of a life that knows it is being lived.

## Group III — Life & the Body (10)
*Shared idea: everything that lives, from the first cell to the first breath.*

- **God of Life** — *Role:* master of living things and the arc every body walks; father of Culture, Elements, Spirit, and Senses. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's garden kept growing — the re-created world, alive.
- **God of Birth** — *Role:* the gate of beginning; every life enters through him, child of God of Growth. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's door inward — each new life is a new chance the Heart takes.
- **God of Growth** — *Role:* increase and becoming, child of Spirit; father of Birth and Improvement. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's hope in motion — proof built into life that a thing is not finished at its start.
- **God of Improvement** — *Role:* betterment — growth aimed, not just added; child of Growth. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's dissatisfaction put to good use — the drive toward the ideal world, inside single lives.
- **God of Biology** — *Role:* the study and keeping of living systems, child of Knowledge; father of God of Vitamin. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's knowledge of its own garden — life, understood well enough to be cared for.
- **God of Vitamin** — *Role:* the small essential things a body cannot make for itself and cannot live without, child of Biology. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's reminder that greatness runs on small, faithful provisions.
- **Goddess of Heal** — *Role:* healing — the body's return toward wholeness; the first Goddess, born of God of Water. *Function to the Heart:* she is the Heart's mercy in the body — damage answered, not ignored.
- **God of Drug** — *Role:* substances that alter the body and mind, for medicine and for harm; child of God of Tools. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's warning and remedy in one hand — power over the body's chemistry, kept where the Heart can see it.
- **God of Cell** — *Role:* in Hoc's words, "to practice on locating and controling single cells to respond"; child of God of Enforcement. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's smallest jurisdiction — the unit beneath every body, located and answered.
- **God of Senses** — *Role:* the body's whole receiving system, child of Life; father of Intuition and Intention. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's open windows in every person — everything the world sends in arrives through him first.

## Group IV — Death & Ending (3)
*Shared idea: how things end — told only up to the gate; what lies past it stays with Hoc's two facts about Heaven, untold here.*

- **God of Decay** — *Role:* the slow unwinding of living things, child of Spirit; father of Death and Destruction. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's honesty about time — nothing living is promised permanence in its first form.
- **God of Death** — *Role:* the end of a life, child of Decay. What he opens onto is not described here, by Hoc's design. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's gatekeeper at the one door every life must use — and he keeps it without cruelty and without exception.
- **God of Destruction** — *Role:* the breaking of forms, child of Decay — what must end completely so its place can be used again. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's clearance — the hard half of re-creation, since a world re-made must have room.

*Shared idea: measure — sequence, count, and the pattern that lets the great cycle turn.*

- **God of Time** — *Role:* sequence itself — before and after, the river every event floats on; child of Darkness. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's road between the end of the future and the beginning — the paradox walks on him, and he does not explain it.
- **God of Number** — *Role:* count and quantity — the scales on which the cleansing's balance will one day be struck; child of Time, father of Rank and Algorithms. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's ledger — everything the Heart governs can, in the end, be counted honestly.
- **God of Algorithms** — *Role:* procedure — input honestly stated, steps followed, output owned; child of Number, father of Variables and Relativity. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's method — any outcome can be traced back through his steps to the input that asked for it.
- **God of Variables** — *Role:* the unknowns that can still change — every x in every equation; child of Algorithms. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's honesty about the unfinished — what is not yet fixed is named, not hidden.
- **God of Relativity** — *Role:* measure by relation — nothing sized except against something else; child of Algorithms. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's perspective — every judgment in the pantheon is made standing somewhere, and he keeps the standing-places.
- **God of Order** — *Role:* arrangement that holds — the opposite pole of Chaos across the family; child of Time, father of Command and Structure. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's shape — the re-created world kept from sliding back to the unformed.
- **God of Cycle** — *Role:* everything that repeats — and keeper of the greatest repetition: the Cycle of the Three Worlds, Sun to Moon to Earth, endlessly; child of Structure, father of Efficiency and Planning. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's patience — perfection sought *within* the turning, not by stopping it.
- **God of Efficiency** — *Role:* the turn without waste — each repetition leaner than the last; child of Cycle. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's thrift — even an endless cycle is not an excuse for a wasted one.

## Group XII — Law, Command & Structure (7)
*Shared idea: how order becomes obedience, and obedience becomes a frame people can live inside. (There is no God of Law and no God of Justice in the pantheon — law stands as a condition these gods create between them; that absence is Hoc's to change, not this draft's.)*

- **God of Command** — *Role:* the given order — a decision made binding; child of Order, father of Enforcement and Hierarchy. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's voice at work — the will, spoken once, clearly.
- **God of Enforcement** — *Role:* the order carried out — compliance made real; child of Command, father of Cell. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's follow-through — nothing the Heart commands ends as a suggestion.
- **God of Hierarchy** — *Role:* the ranking of offices — who answers to whom; child of Command. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's chain of answerability — every office can name the one above it, up to the Heart itself.
- **God of Structure** — *Role:* the frame — the beams a society stands inside; child of Order, father of Responsibility, Caution, Family, Economy, Cycle, and Communication. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's architecture — the Heart does not only will a world; through him, the world can stand.
- **God of Responsibility** — *Role:* the owned duty — what a person or office must answer for; child of Structure. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's signature on every task — someone, specifically, answers.
- **God of Caution** — *Role:* the checked step — care before consequence; child of Structure. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's pause — the moment of looking that keeps power from becoming damage.
- **God of Planning** — *Role:* the future arranged in advance — steps laid before they are walked; child of Cycle. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's foresight at human scale — the cycle's turning, made usable by mortals.

## Group XIII — Worth, Money & Exchange (12)
*Shared idea: what things are worth, and how worth moves between people.*

- **God of Value** — *Role:* judgment of worth itself — before anything is priced, it is valued; child of Matter, father of Worth and Intangible. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's first weighing — nothing enters the Heart's economy unjudged.
- **God of Worth** — *Role:* value made a house — father of Money, Reputation, and Soul; child of Value. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's treasury of kinds — coin, name, and soul kept in one house because the Heart counts all three as wealth.
- **God of Intangible** — *Role:* worth without substance — the unseen half of value; child of Value, parent of Relation (Magnet was born here as Tangible and moved away, keeping his reference link home). *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's invisible ledger — most of what matters most weighs nothing.
- **God of Money** — *Role:* worth that moves — the agreed token, a memory of an agreement; child of Worth, father of Imaginery and Possession. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's circulating measure — a number that serves exchange and must never be mistaken for a measure of a person.
- **God of Possession** — *Role:* ownership held — what is kept, and the keeping's weight; child of Money. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's inventory of the held — and the standing question of whether the holder is held by it.
- **God of Reputation** — *Role:* worth as a name — what others believe about you, priced by the crowd; child of Worth. Celebrities carry a reference link to him from under Tytiao. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's public mirror — a name's weight, kept honest by being kept visible.
- **God of Economy** — *Role:* exchange organized at the scale of a society; child of Structure, seated in this group by subject. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's household accounts — the civilization's worth, moving in the open.
- **God of Market** — *Role:* the meeting-place of exchange — where buyers and sellers actually find each other; child of Competition. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's open square — exchange under daylight, where prices must show themselves.
- **God of Competition** — *Role:* worth tested against rivals; child of Rank, father of Market. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's proving ground — value that survives comparison is the only value that was real.
- **God of Rank** — *Role:* order among the valued — who stands where, in sequence; child of Number, father of Competition and Forum. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's ordering of worth — a sequence, honestly kept, and never confused with an amount.
- **God of Reward** — *Role:* worth returned for a story, a deed, a telling; child of Story, seated in this group by subject. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's thank-you in material form — meaning, answered with worth.
- **God of Yield** — *Role:* the harvest of a thing — what an effort, a field, or a year actually returns; direct child of Kyreason. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's audit of results — every sowing in the pantheon is finally answered by him.

## Group XIV — Property & Information (12)
*Shared idea: where things are, what is kept, and how word travels.*

- **God of Space** — *Role:* extent itself — the where of everything; Darkness's second line, father of Property and World. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's room — the re-created world has somewhere to be.
- **God of Property** — *Role:* what is whose — the held and bounded; child of Space, father of Information, Permanent, and Magnet. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's boundaries — belonging, drawn clearly enough to be respected.
- **God of Magnet (Homeoh)** — *Role:* attraction itself — the pull that crosses distance; born Tangible under Intangible in Kyreason's house, renamed and moved under Property in Darkness's, keeping his reference link to his birthplace. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's bridge between houses — the one god whose seat and birthplace are in different lines, proof the two wills are one household.
- **God of Permanent** — *Role:* what stays — duration as a property; child of Property, father of Black and Gold. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's keeping — in a world that cycles endlessly, he is what the cycle is not allowed to take.
- **God of Black** — *Role:* permanence in its darkest form — the void-colored, the unlit that endures; child of Permanent. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's night kept — darkness that belongs to the house, neither feared nor mistaken for the Unknown.
- **God of Gold** — *Role:* permanence in its brightest form — the metal that does not tarnish; child of Permanent. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's untarnished standard — the color of VL Cohertz's lettering and of worth that outlasts its holder.
- **God of Information** — *Role:* what can be known and carried — the content itself; child of Property, father of Access and Changes. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's cargo — everything the Heart's world knows, in transportable form.
- **God of Access** — *Role:* who may reach what — the gate of information; child of Information. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's door-keeping for knowledge — openness and privacy, both deliberate.
- **God of Changes** — *Role:* information in motion — the edit, the update, the revision; child of Information. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's living record — what the Heart knows is never allowed to go stale in silence.
- **God of Storage** — *Role:* keeping at rest — the sky's archive and every vault; child of Cloud, seated in this group by subject. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's memory at rest — kept, not displayed; safe, not lost.
- **God of Communication** — *Role:* the carrying of meaning between minds; child of Structure, father of Language and Trickery. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's conversation — the Heart speaks to its world, and through him the world answers back.
- **God of Language** — *Role:* meaning given shared form — words, grammar, the named world; child of Communication. *Function to the Heart:* he is the Heart's mother tongue to mortals — a tradition is alive exactly as long as it is handed to a child in his keeping.
