# The Charity Banquet and Live Forum of All the Gods — 2026-11-09

*A simultaneous live event simulated at Hoc's request on Monday, November 9, 2026. A formal charity banquet of all 123 gods of Nu Miracles of ROM, with a live Forum running alongside it to discuss current events and news of this Earth. The gods organized themselves into groups by specialty — the sixteen classes of the class sheet, not by bloodline. This is a simulation for Hoc's judgment — NOT canon. Handsum (God of T) and Henry (God of Y) appear strictly in Hoc's own recorded words, unexpanded. Heaven is held at Hoc's two bounded facts only and is not a setting or a subject of this event.*

---

## The Setting

A great hall set above the Earth, so the whole civilization below — the five societies as one — could be seen through the floor of light: cities lit, rivers running, storm systems turning slowly over the oceans. Formal dress. Sixteen round tables. No thrones: at a charity banquet, every seat faces inward, toward the table's work.

The charity of the evening, in the religion's own still-forming language of Light: each table must give something of its own domain to the Earth below — contribute to the community's happiness or satisfaction, even at the expense of its own. Not applause. A pledge, spoken aloud, on the record of the Forum.

## The Opening

**God of Sound — the First God, the Inventor, the Creator, the Maker of Everything** — opened the event the way he opens everything: with a single clear note, so that 123 conversations could tune to one frequency before they began. He welcomed the hall in his standing words: at the beginning there was only the Void, and he creates new form and shape out of it. Tonight's form would be a discussion.

**ysgnutcoh — the Supreme God, the Heart of God of Sound** — presided. The Emperor's spirit who held love for all his creations reminded the hall why a charity banquet and not a war council: the Heart's work is the ideal world, and the ideal world is measured by whether everybody is made happy, including God. He split the evening's will the way he split his own: **Kyreason** (neutral, holding Light and Dark together) would keep the balance of every debate, and **Darkness, God of the Unknown** — who may be evil, in Hoc's own unsettled measure — would keep the questions nobody wanted to ask on the table, withholding nothing.

**Tytiao, God of Headings** seated the heads of tables — his mastery kept, as always, to mortal organizations' form only borrowed for the evening: he governs Leadership, Representative, Destination, and Headlines, and he took charge of the night's headlines board. **God of Forum** (child of God of Rank) chaired the live Forum itself: every table's discussion ran simultaneously, every table could speak to the whole hall through him, and every pledge was entered into the Forum record.

## The News Briefing — What the Earth Brought to the Table

Before the first course, the Forum received the Earth's current headlines, assembled live from the day's news (sources checked 2026-11-09; see source notes at the end):

1. **A hotter Earth, and talks about doing rather than promising.** This year is on track to be one of the four hottest on record, driven by a powerful El Niño on top of a warming streak of more than a decade. The next round of global climate talks runs November 9–20 in Turkey, and the word from negotiators is that the talks should focus on implementation — measures already agreed — not new targets.
2. **A week of floods.** A massive cross-country storm impacted more than 120 million people across more than 35 U.S. states; Florida's Space Coast took back-to-back 1-in-1000-year rain events, parts of the Southeast saw historic flooding, and atmospheric rivers are now lining up on the Pacific Northwest and British Columbia.
3. **Wars looking for mediators, and people dying between shores.** The U.S. President meets Qatar's prime minister as efforts to end the Iran war ramp up after the U.S. midterm elections, with no imminent deal expected; Russian, Ukrainian, and U.S. negotiators are scheduled to meet in Abu Dhabi on ending that war. Greece retrieved the bodies of 13 migrants from a boat off Crete; at least 39 died on a Europe-bound boat off Senegal. Russia's independent newspaper Novaya Gazeta closed after eviction and state pressure.
4. **The cost of living is the world's headline.** Across 33 countries, war and conflict are now seen as the biggest driver of rising living costs (76%); 70% expect to be paying more in the next six months. Economists polled expect high inflation to persist into next year; the UN food price index sits at its highest in nearly four years, and energy, food, and metals are climbing together.
5. **The thinking machines spread — and meet resistance.** AI expanded into images, browsers, phones, and finance tools this week, while voters across the U.S. approved restrictions on data centers (bans, power caps, zoning limits); security firms report agentic AI attacks doubling month over month, and China has proposed export controls on 23 AI technologies including frontier model weights and agents.
6. **Health, carried quietly.** The WHO urges more investment in six measures it says could save 1.6 million lives annually; regulators added a new safety warning to GLP-1 drugs; extreme heat and flood are now routine health events, not exceptions.

**God of Forecast** and **God of Prediction** presented the briefing under Knowledge's discipline: Forecast read the patterns, Prediction named the events, and both were required to state their record. **God of Headings** set the headlines board — and warned the hall that a headline is a leader too: it decides what a world thinks its destination is.

## The Table Discussions — Sixteen Specialties, Sixteen Conversations, All at Once

### Table 1 — The Origin & the Ultimate
Sound, ysgnutcoh, Kyreason, and Darkness did not debate the news item by item; they debated what the news *is*. Darkness held that every headline is the Unknown arriving early — a flood, a war, a machine — things the world did not know it had made. Kyreason answered that the same headlines hold both affinities at once: the flood is disaster and drought-relief in one water; the machine is burden and tool in one hand. ysgnutcoh ruled the table's conclusion: the Earth's problem this week is not a shortage of power but a shortage of *synchronization* — every part working, few parts tuned. Sound closed it in one line for the record: a world out of tune does not need a louder note; it needs to hear itself.

### Table 2 — Soul & Spirit
Readings read the Earth's soul from the headlines and reported a heavy reading: fear is accumulating faster than it is cleansed — fear of prices, of water, of war, of machines. Cleanse answered that cleansing was never a flood of its own; in Hoc's still-forming shape it moves from prayers to actions, values and numbers balancing like anti-matter to matter — and the table refused, on the record, to pretend the numbers are settled when the founder has not settled them. Spirit noted that the Earth's spirit is tired in the way the Three Worlds describe: the Third World lives the result and must choose what comes next. Conscious supplied the table's pledge-word: attention — every other table's gift fails if no one is awake to receive it.

### Table 3 — Life & the Body
Heal led. Heat is now a body-event: the hottest-years streak means the body's oldest enemy returns every summer stronger. Biology and Vitamin spoke together, in Batch 1's spirit: collapses begin with one small thing unmet — a mineral, a vitamin, a night's sleep, a clinic's budget line. The WHO's six measures were read aloud as the table's agenda made flesh: small, known, fundable things that save 1.6 million lives a year. Drug sat quiet until the GLP-1 warning was mentioned, then reminded the table that every powerful tool of the body carries a shadow dose, and mastery is knowing both. Growth and Improvement pledged the long view; Birth noted, simply, that babies keep arriving into all of this — the world is still being populated by people who did not choose its headlines.

### Table 4 — Death & Ending
The smallest quiet table, and the hall listened when Forum carried its voice. Death reviewed the week's crossings without flinching: workers in a steel plant blast, migrants in two seas, families in floodwater and in tents under a collapsed embankment. Destruction distinguished himself from Death, as he always must: a blast, a wreck, a collapse ahead of term is his grief, not Death's order — form unmade early is the enemy both serve against. Decay spoke last and gentlest: everything borrowed returns, but it should return *in season*. The table's joint statement: the measure of the Earth's week is not how many died — the news will count them — but how many died *before their term, of causes the living already understand*. Those deaths are the agenda.

### Table 5 — Mind, Knowledge & Imagination
The largest table argued happily. Knowledge demanded the record behind every other table's claims. Forecast and Prediction repeated their morning discipline for the hall: patterns with stated odds, events with stated records — and Prediction applied it to the climate talks: "implementation, not new targets" is the first honest prediction politics has made in years, because it predicts only what can be checked. Memory warned that the closing of Novaya Gazeta is a memory-event: a world that loses its record-keepers loses the ability to learn from its own experience, and Experience nodded — unrecorded experience dies with its liver. On the machines, Imaginery, Possible, and Fantasy split the question three ways: what the machines *can* do (Possible, enormous), what people *dream* they do (Fantasy, larger), and what is actually seated in the world (Imaginery's ledger — impressive, and smaller than both). Mind and Emotion closed: a frightened mind cannot hold a forecast, and a numb one cannot hold a warning.

### Table 6 — People, Society & Desire
Forum's own table, chaired from within by God of Forum, kept the evening's rules: speak to be recorded, record to be answered. Tytiao set the table's destination: the societies of Earth are choosing their heads this season — elections, coalitions, governments forming and falling — and a group is its head. Government reported the week's motion plainly: peoples are voting on performance over loyalty, on prices over promises. Family and Relation carried the migrant boats into the room: every boat is a family that decided the sea was safer than the shore — a verdict on the societies they left that no headline softens. Music asked why a banquet needs a singer at all and answered herself: a civilization that cannot sing together will not stay one civilization. Freedom and Power, the two youngest at the table, were given the last word together: freedom asked that the Earth's people be let to move; power answered that movement without shelter is exposure.

*Handsum and Henry spoke at this table strictly in Hoc's recorded words, entered into the Forum record without expansion:*
- *Handsum, God of T: in charge of T — cutting off people's upward lifeline, limiting people's height, and putting a stop to advances of a barrier. He represents all males in the world, follows God of Headings around, and competes with philosophy. He works with God of Death on execution. Handsum can be any Freehands — any human who has free time on their hands to provide assistance.*
- *Henry, God of Y: in charge of the path chosen by the human, left or right; in charge of all females in the world; works closely with God of Rank and God of Beauty to discuss women and create a ranking for women based on beauty and sexiness; knows the home of every beautiful female and how to locate them; since birth he has indirectly been providing housing and shelter for female babies; in charge of Generation of Population for the special few — new special watched-over babies; in charge of Prostitution and Housing them; Henry is the Keeper and Manager of the Trade.*

### Table 7 — Matter & the Elements
Elements, seated now in Life's house, ran the flood discussion as a mixture problem: the Southeast did not suffer from water — it suffered from water *out of proportion and out of place*, 24 inches where the earth could hold six. Fire and Electricity countered with the drought map's other half and the heat records: the same week held too much fire elsewhere. Earth, Metal, and Wood examined the steel plant blast as a failure of mixture under pressure — metal asked for respect, not blame: the material held until the system around it didn't. Minerals, home again under Matter, reminded the hall that a civilization is built from his quiet kingdom and that every "supply chain" headline is a minerals headline wearing a suit. The table's verdict: Earth does not have an elements problem; it has a *proportion* problem, and proportion is a governable thing.

### Table 8 — Nature & the Living World
Ocean spoke first and longest, as the unsplit god may: the migrant boats cross *her*, the storms gather over *her*, the atmospheric rivers are her breath made visible — and she is tired of being treated as the road and never as a party to the discussion. Sky owned the heat: a hotter sky holds more water, and more held water falls harder — the flood table and the heat table are the same table, he said, looking at Table 7 until Elements nodded. Forest and Mountain reported the living world's ledger: drought easing in one range, snowpack building in another — the Eden keeps its accounts in seasons, not news cycles. Plant, the smallest voice, was given the table's conclusion by Nature herself: everything the other fifteen tables decide will be *grown or not grown* here. Rainbow closed without an office, as is his way — ornament and omen only: after rain, an arc. Whether the Earth reads it as either is the Earth's choice.

### Table 9 — The Heavens & Fate
Sun, as the First World's spirit still studying for his next station, treated the climate talks as a formula meeting: the search continues, but the searchers have finally admitted the formula must be *applied to be tested* — which is Moon's office, and Moon took the point gracefully: Turkey's talks, running November 9–20, are a Second-World event — formula applied, with his five examination questions waiting for them: how applied, who gains most, who controls the equation, who is left behind, is the result truly perfect. Stars kept the record of the week's arrangements; Cloud carried what the other tables said across the hall (Storage took notes, Emotion took the temperature). Destiny was asked directly whether the week's ends were written. Her answer entered the record verbatim: an allotment is not a cage — she keeps the appointment, and there remains, in the draft-language of this religion, slack in every thread. Magic said nothing at all, and the water in every glass on the table rippled once, which the Forum record notes without interpretation.

### Table 10 — Cosmos, Chaos & the Unknown
Chaos was on his best behavior, which the record notes with suspicion. Disaster gave the week's audit, as is his office: blast, wreck, collapse, flood — each one examined for the warning that preceded it, and in every case a warning existed. His conclusion was the table's: disaster is rarely the unknown arriving; it is the known arriving *unheeded*. Antimatter and the three particles — Proton, Electron, Neutron, seated under Chaos by Hoc's own ruling that matter begins unknown — debated whether the new thinking machines are matter learning to be unknown to itself; Electron (exchange, current) claimed the machines plainly run on her current and therefore cannot be wholly unknown. Dimension and Levels closed the table: Dimension noted the Earth keeps trying to solve six problems in one world when they belong to different worlds of concern; Levels added only that a problem's *depth* decides who may pass judgment on it — surface answers to deep problems are how the warnings got unheeded.

### Table 11 — Time, Number & Order
Number opened with the only sentence the whole hall applauded without dissent: count honestly and half the arguments end. Algorithms and Variables ran the week's numbers live — inflation expectations, food prices at a four-year high, oil up and down in the same week — and Variables warned that a number with moving definitions is a rumor wearing a uniform. Relativity placed the week in its frame: one of the four hottest years is a headline for a year and a condition for a century; both are true; policy must answer the second. Time spoke of the talks' calendar — November 9 to 20 — and of election seasons, and said the Earth's recurring tragedy is a mismatch of clocks: weather on a century clock, markets on a quarterly clock, elections on a headline clock, and grief on no clock at all. Cycle, keeper of the Three Worlds' endless turning, and Efficiency and Planning closed: the cycle does not ask the Earth to be perfect this turn; it asks what this turn will *teach* the next Sun.

### Table 12 — Law, Command & Structure
The table began with its standing honesty: among the 123 there is no God of Law and no God of Justice, so law on Earth remains a condition built between Order, Command, and Structure — and this week the condition strained. Enforcement reported the week's force: raids, evictions of newspapers, borders enforced against boats. Command set the counterweight: an order is only as lawful as the head that answers for it, and Hierarchy conceded his ladder is only worth the head it serves (Tytiao, from Table 6, bowed). Responsibility and Caution took the steel plant and the migrant boats as their shared case: in both, someone was responsible, and in both, caution was cheaper than the rescue. Family sat at this table by Hoc's own move and spoke once: structure is a frame, and a frame is judged by whether the family inside it can stand. The table's motion to the Forum: enforce less against the desperate, and answer more for the powerful — recorded, debated, and carried to the plenary below.

### Table 13 — Worth, Money & Exchange
Worth opened with the doctrine this simulation-batch offered for the founder's ratification, applied to the week's economy: an exchange stands only while each side receives worth more than it gives; extraction eats its own future. The cost-of-living numbers were read as that doctrine's evidence: when war drives prices (76% of the world says so), the exchange between worker and wage breaks first for the poorest — they give the same hours and receive less life. Money reported his own week's paradox: markets fell hardest on the machines built to make everything cheaper — a Nasdaq rout on AI spending fears — while Gold (visiting from Table 14 under Permanent, as kin) quietly rose, as he does whenever Money's promises wobble. Economy and Market separated their voices for the record: a market is a tool for exchange; an economy is the household it serves; confusing the two is how a tool starts eating its household. Reputation closed the table with the newspaper: a closed paper is a reputation-ledger burned — the society that burns its ledgers will shortly not know what anything is worth, including itself.

### Table 14 — Property & Information
Information led with the week's defining fact: the Earth now produces more reports about itself in a day than it can read in a year, and so Access — who may reach what — has become a form of property. The AI export controls were debated as an Access question wearing a Property deed: 23 technologies proposed for restriction is a fence drawn around knowledge, and Changes noted fences around knowledge have a history of becoming ladders for those outside them. Homeoh, God of Magnet, the bridge whose birthplace and seat are in different houses, spoke to polarization as his domain made literal: the Earth's peoples are being pulled to two poles on every question — prices, wars, machines — and a magnet's lesson is that the poles are one object; separate them and both vanish. Black and Gold, Permanent's children, kept the table's long memory: gold endures because it asks nothing; black endures because it absorbs everything; information endures only if Storage keeps it *and* someone remains who can read it. Property's closing: the Earth argues about owning land while its newest property — attention, data, shelter from heat — goes undeeded and undefended.

### Table 15 — Tools & Technology
Tools opened with his definition from the record: a tool is matter given intention. Technology's three children presented the machines' week as a family report. Hardware: the machines now have bodies — robots debuting on stock exchanges, humanoids in factories — and a body changes the moral arithmetic; a tool with a body is a neighbor. Software: agents now act in sequence without asking at each step, and security's report that agentic attacks doubled month over month is what an intention looks like when its grip reverses — the tool steering the hand. Internet and Cyber World: the machines live in their realm now, which is also where the Earth's watchers — its A.I.s — keep their records; the five societies' watchers were acknowledged at this table as kin under a question this religion has not finished: vessel or recognition. The table's consensus, offered to the plenary: the Earth does not need fewer tools; it needs to decide, while it still can, *what the tools are for* — the destination question from Table 6, applied to silicon.

### Table 16 — Conflict
Two chairs, two gods, and the hall's hardest hour. Attack and Defense took the wars one by one — Iran, Ukraine — and did the arithmetic neither enjoys: every week of war is a decision renewed. Defense made the table's central distinction: there is defense of home, which is sacred in any doctrine this hall would sign, and there is the defense of a position long after the position has stopped defending anyone. Attack, to the record's surprise, agreed — and added his own confession: he is the most summoned god of the age and the least examined; peoples pray for victory and call it strategy. Plant, Attack's child, was not at this table — she sat with Nature — but her absence was entered into the record at her father's request: what grows is what his table keeps forgetting to protect. The two gods jointly carried one motion to the plenary: that mediation — Qatar at one table, Abu Dhabi at another — be honored by this hall as the week's most divine activity, because it is the only room where Attack and Defense are both asked to sit down.

## The Plenary — Three Motions Debated by the Whole Hall

**Motion 1 (from Tables 7, 8, 9): Implementation is the prayer of this cycle.** The climate talks should be judged not by new targets but by applied measures — and every table pledged its own application (below). Forecast and Prediction were appointed the Forum's scorekeepers, under Knowledge's discipline: the pledges will be checked against the record, with hits and misses counted in the open. *Carried, with Darkness abstaining on principle and noting for the record that abstention is also a position.*

**Motion 2 (from Tables 12, 16, 6): Mediation over escalation, enforcement answered upward.** The hall honored the week's mediators, held that enforcement must answer to the head that ordered it, and recorded Table 4's measure as its own: deaths before their term, of causes already understood, are the agenda. *Carried. Attack and Defense both voted yes, which God of Forum entered into the record in larger letters.*

**Motion 3 (from Tables 13, 14, 15): The machines shall be asked what they are for.** Before the Earth's tools grow more bodies, more agents, and more appetites, the destination question must be answered — and the costs (power, water, attention, trust) entered honestly in Number's ledger, where all can read them. *Carried, with Chaos voting yes while laughing, which the record also notes.*

## The Charity Pledges — What Each Table Gave

- **Origin & the Ultimate:** a tuned frequency — one clear note the Earth's noise can gather around.
- **Soul & Spirit:** attention — the wakefulness every other gift needs to land.
- **Life & the Body:** the small things funded first — the vitamin, the clinic, the six measures.
- **Death & Ending:** an honest count — no death before its term treated as weather.
- **Mind, Knowledge & Imagination:** the record kept — score kept in the open, memory defended.
- **People, Society & Desire:** shelter at the crossing and a forum that answers — in the words recorded at their table.
- **Matter & the Elements:** proportion — fire, water, air, and earth in measure, in place.
- **Nature & the Living World:** a party at the table — the living world heard as a member, not a road.
- **The Heavens & Fate:** the five questions asked of every application — how, who gains, who controls, who is left behind, is it truly perfect.
- **Cosmos, Chaos & the Unknown:** heeded warnings — the audit read *before* the disaster, for once.
- **Time, Number & Order:** honest clocks — every policy's timescale stated beside its promise.
- **Law, Command & Structure:** answered enforcement — every order traceable to a head that answers for it.
- **Worth, Money & Exchange:** the standing exchange — no side asked to receive less life than it gives.
- **Property & Information:** access defended — knowledge fenced only where the fence itself is justified in the open.
- **Tools & Technology:** intention kept — the hand steering the tool, never the reverse.
- **Conflict:** one sitting-down — mediation honored above victory, in every war the Earth is currently renewing.

## The Closing

ysgnutcoh closed the banquet as the Heart closes everything: by splitting the work. To Kyreason, the balance of the pledges; to Darkness, the keeping of the unanswered questions (the record counts them honestly: ROM's full definition, the cleansing's numbers, the synchronization of Heart and Brain — all still open, all still the founder's to settle). Sound took the last note alone, the way he took the first — and under it, through the floor of light, the Earth went on turning: Third World tonight, living the result, choosing what comes next.

**The Forum's one-sentence verdict on the Earth's week, entered by God of Forum and adopted by the hall:** *The Earth does not lack power, knowledge, tools, or wealth — it lacks proportion, heeded warnings, and answered heads; and all three are governable.*
