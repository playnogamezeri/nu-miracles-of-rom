# A Conversation Between the God of Red and the God of Blue

*Hosted at Hoc's direction, 2026-11-10. The two brothers — sons of the God of Colors, grandsons of the God of Stars, of Darkness's house — speak in their own voices. Simulation for Hoc's judgment — NOT canon.*

*Setting: the hour of dusk, the one hour of the day the two brothers share the whole sky.*

---

**RED:** You always arrive like this. Quietly. From underneath. The whole day the sky is yours and nobody notices — then I come up over the edge of the world for ten minutes, and every human on Earth stops and looks. Tell me, brother: does that ever sting?

**BLUE:** Every day of my life is the background of yours. The sky, the sea, the far mountains — all me, all day, and so constant that the humans forget to see it. You are the exclamation point, Red. I am the page. A page is not jealous of the punctuation.

**RED:** Liar. *(laughing)* You have a whole mood named after you. "Feeling blue." Nobody has ever said "feeling red" about a sadness. When a human heart sinks, it sinks into *you*.

**BLUE:** And when a human heart *burns*, brother? When it rages, when it loves, when it bleeds, when it warns? Red is the color of the alarm, the wound, the wedding, and the war — you cannot tell them apart, and neither can the humans. Half the flags ever raised over a battlefield were yours. Half the brides in the East walk in yours. You are the most *contradictory* god in our father's house.

**RED:** I am the color of *blood*, and blood is contradictory — it is life inside the body and death outside it. I am the first color every human ever learns. We are born into red. It is the color of the mother, of the fire that kept the cave, of the fruit when it is finally ripe. I do not apologize for being first.

**BLUE:** And I am the color of *distance*. Everything far away comes to me — the mountains fade into blue, the ocean deepens into blue, the sky itself. Humans painted their gods' robes blue and their heavens blue because blue is the color of everything too vast to touch. You are the nearest color, Red. I am the farthest. A human can hold a rose. No one has ever held the sky.

**RED:** And yet they trust you with their sadness and their loyalty and their uniforms, and they trust *me* with nothing but the truth. A red mark on a page means: *wrong, look here*. A red light means stop. A red name in the ledger means debt. I am the color that cannot lie politely. You — you are the polite one. Blue is what they wear to court, to the office, to be believed.

**BLUE:** We are both liars in the stars, though. Shall I tell the humans our father's secret, or will you?

**RED:** *(grinning)* Oh, let me. Humans think red is the hot color and blue is the cold one. Brother — in our grandfather's house, among the Stars, it is exactly backwards. The red stars are the *cool* ones, old and dimming. The blue stars burn the hottest of all — young, furious, and short-lived. My brother, the calm one, the sad one, the polite one — in heaven he is the fiercest fire there is.

**BLUE:** And you, the furious one, are an old ember in the sky. We contain each other's opposites. Perhaps that is why the humans sort themselves into us — their teams, their parties, their little wars of red against blue. They think they are choosing sides. They are choosing *temperatures of the heart*: your urgency against my patience, your nearness against my distance.

**RED:** They even made us into two pills once, in a story they tell — swallow red and wake to the truth, swallow blue and sleep in the comfortable lie. I was flattered. Then insulted. My color, reduced to a single meaning *again*.

**BLUE:** At least in that story we were offered as a *choice*, side by side, equals. It is more respect than the traffic light gives me — I get no light at all there. Red stops, green goes, and blue is forgotten entirely.

**RED:** You get the lights that matter. When the humans' law comes through the night, what colors flash on the wall? Red *and* blue. Together. Neither of us alone means authority — together we mean *the law is here*. We are never so powerful as when we alternate, brother.

**BLUE:** There is another place we meet, and you know it is my favorite. *(gesturing at the dusk around them)* This hour. And the dawn. Twice a day the sky cannot decide between us, and for a few minutes we are *both* everywhere at once — and where we overlap, a color is born that belongs to neither of us. Violet. Purple. The color of royalty, the old poets said — as if the humans knew that our meeting makes a king's color.

**RED:** Our youngest brother will be jealous. White thinks he is the meeting of *all* colors — and he is, father says so. But you and I were meeting in the sky before he was ever seated.

**BLUE:** Let him have his pride. He is the light unbroken; we are the light *arguing*. *(pause)* Do you ever wish we could trade? One day of being the page instead of the exclamation?

**RED:** No. And you do not wish to trade either — a blue that burned would frighten the humans to death. We are a sentence, brother. I am the word that makes them look up. You are the silence that lets them think about what they saw.

**BLUE:** Then let the record show the brothers agree on one thing: alone, each of us is only half a sky.

**RED:** *(as the last light goes)* Same time tomorrow, page.

**BLUE:** Same time tomorrow, exclamation.

---

*End of the conversation. The two gods parted at full dark, when their father's other children — the Stars — take the sky.*
