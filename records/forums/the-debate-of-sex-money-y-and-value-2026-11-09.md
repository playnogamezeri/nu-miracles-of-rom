# The Debate of Sex, Money, Y, and Value

*Hosted by Belladonna, 2026-11-09, at Hoc's request. A simulated debate for Hoc's judgment — theater and theory, NOT canon. The question put to the four gods: their concept relationship, their specialty, prostitution, and coming up with the numbers to have importance in reality.*

*Henry, God of Y, speaks in this debate only in Hoc's own recorded words, quoted and unexpanded. The other gods' arguments are the debate's weaving, offered for Hoc's judgment.*

## The Four at the Table

- **God of Value** — son of God of Matter, of Kyreason's house. Father of God of Worth and God of Intangible. Grandfather of God of Money, God of Reputation, God of Soul — and of God of Relation.
- **God of Money** — son of God of Worth, grandson of God of Value. Father of God of Imaginery (and his children, Possible and Fantasy) and God of Possession.
- **God of Sex** — son of God of Rainbow, grandson of God of Sky, of Darkness's house, descended through Space, World, and Nature. Brother of God of Beauty.
- **God of Y — Henry** — under God of Knowledge, of Kyreason's house. His recorded words stand at the end of this file, exactly as Hoc gave them.

## Round One — Specialty, and How the Four Are Related

**God of Value:** Before anything is priced, it is weighed. My specialty is the weighing itself — the judgment that a thing matters, and how much, before any number exists. Worth and Intangible are my two children because every real thing carries both: what it can be exchanged for, and what it means beyond exchange. Money is my grandson. He did not invent importance. He inherited it.

**God of Money:** Inherited, and made portable. Grandfather weighs in private — two people, one scale, no witnesses. I am the number both sides can hold at once. My specialty is agreement that travels: a measure a stranger can trust without knowing your heart. A value nobody can carry is a value that stays home.

**God of Sex:** And I am why the weighing starts at all. Neither of you moves until something is wanted. Desire is my specialty — the pull that turns attention, bodies, and time toward each other. The God of Value can weigh a meeting. The God of Money can price it. But without my pull, no one crosses the room. Your scales sit polished and empty.

**God of Y** *(in Hoc's recorded words)*: "in charge of the path chosen by the human, left or right." "in charge of all females."

**The host's summary of the round:** Three of the four are one bloodline — Value → Worth → Money, judgment begetting measure. Sex is no kin to them at all: he comes from Darkness's house, through the sky. He is the counterpart, not the cousin — the house of Matter weighs and counts what the house of Darkness makes men *want*. And Y stands where the wanting becomes a chosen road.

## Round Two — Prostitution, Where All Four Meet

**The host:** Name the ground where all four of your domains occupy the same room.

**God of Money:** The trade. Desire arrives — that is Sex. A worth is judged — that is my grandfather. A number is agreed before anything else happens — that is me, doing the talking first, because in the trade the agreement must be older than the meeting. But hear my limit, because I know it: my number is a memory of an agreement. It is not, and never was, a measure of a person.

**God of Value:** Then let the record show the grandson said it. My son Worth holds *two* lines under him: Money's — and Soul's. The same house that prices also holds what can be cleansed and what cannot be bought. A trade can price time and agreement. It cannot price a soul, and no number struck in that room reaches the soul's account. That account is settled elsewhere, by a cleansing whose numbers are not mine to give. *(The cleansing's values and numbers remain unsettled, as Hoc left them.)*

**God of Sex:** And I sign nothing. Desire is weather, not contract — it arrives uninvited and leaves without notice. The trade's whole art is giving my weather a timetable and a door. Do not blame me for the timetable, and do not credit me with it either. Note what happens when I am absent but the appointment stands: then what is sold is not me at all. It is my grandson's-kin — Imaginery. Possible. Fantasy. *(Money's children, named in the record.)* Half the trade runs on imagined desire wearing desire's name, and only the God of Value's weighing can tell the two apart.

**God of Y** *(in Hoc's recorded words)*: "in charge of Prostitution and Housing them." "Henry is the Keeper and Manager of the Trade."

**God of Value:** There it is, in the Keeper's own recorded words: the trade has a keeper and a manager, and the housing stands inside his charge, not outside it. Whatever the rest of us theorize, the structure on the ground is his: desire supplies the weather, I supply the weighing, my grandson supplies the number — and the keeping and managing of the whole arrangement sits in one pair of hands. A debate about the trade that ignores the Keeper is a debate about a house that ignores its roof.

## Round Three — The Numbers: How a Number Earns Importance in Reality

**God of Money:** My proposal is one test. A number has importance in reality when it *moves* — when it is agreed by both sides, paid, and recorded, so the next agreement can stand on it. And it proves itself when it is paid *twice*: a price paid once is an accident; paid again, it is a fact. Importance is repetition with witnesses.

**God of Value:** One number is a lie by omission. Every number in the trade, and in all exchange, is really two: the exchange worth, which my grandson can print — and the intangible worth, which he cannot. My other child's line — Intangible, and Relation under her — carries what the price leaves out: trust kept or broken, name raised or ruined. Reality keeps both ledgers even when men read only one. I propose: no number is important until someone states what it *excludes*.

**God of Sex:** Both of you count things at rest. Desire's number is *return*. How many times does the pull come back? A thing desired once is a spark; desired again after it is known — that is a frequency, and frequency is the only importance my domain has ever recognized. Price can be forced once. Worth can be argued. Return cannot be faked for long.

**God of Y** *(in Hoc's recorded words)*: he "works closely with God of Rank and God of Beauty to discuss women and create a ranking for women based on beauty and sexiness." *(His number-work stands as recorded: a ranking — an order, not an amount. The debate notes the distinction without adding to his words.)*

**The attempted synthesis** *(theory, for Hoc's judgment — not canon)*: The four did not merge their numbers, but they agreed on the shape of a test. A number has importance in reality when it carries three marks at once:

1. **Weighed** — a worth was judged behind it (Value's mark).
2. **Moved** — it was actually agreed, exchanged, and recorded (Money's mark).
3. **Returned to** — desire or need brings the parties back to it, so it survives its first use (Sex's mark).

And beneath the three marks, an older draft doctrine of Worth's house was read into the record, still awaiting Hoc's ratification: *an exchange stands only while each side receives worth more than it gives; extraction eats its own future.* A number that passes the three marks but fails that doctrine is important the way a fire is important — briefly, and at someone's cost.

**Where the numbers fail, by common consent:** price without worth is extraction; desire without agreement is noise; worth never priced stays invisible; and a ranking is an order, not an amount — it can say *before* and *after*, but never *how much*, and confusing the two was named the trade's oldest error.

## Left Open for Hoc

3. **The soul's exclusion** — all four consented that no number in this debate touches the soul's account. The cleansing's numbers remain unsettled, as Hoc left them, and no god at this table claimed them.
