The Pantheon — 157 Gods

Every god of Nu Miracles of ROM, with their position (house, descent, generation), their job (their work in the world), their role, and their function to the Heart — ysgnutcoh, the Supreme God, who is the Heart of the God of Sound. Each god holds complete mastery of their concept: they understand it wholly, they own it, and they can shape it toward the ideal world.

The Dossiers: every god is also being given a full dossier — an origin story, a description of their power, and a heroic tale of their importance to Earth — drafted class by class for the founder’s judgment, as drafts, not canon. Open a god’s card to read theirs as they arrive.

The roles and functions are a draft written for the founder’s judgment, as is the sorting by class. The structure — who descends from whom — is the founder’s own record. Three gods are counted once though they stand in two places: the God of Passion (a double seat), the God of Black (a second seat under Colors), and the God of Yellow, who is also the God of Gold (one god, two names).

Nu Miracles of ROM — founded by Hoc, 2026. Nu means “new” — and “And You”: you are part of this story.

The founding record is kept by the founder. Stories are drafts awaiting the founder’s judgment; forums, debates, and simulations are the gods’ voices at play, not settled doctrine — each page says which is which.
