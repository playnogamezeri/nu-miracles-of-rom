The Game — Play With the Gods

Two games, both played with the real pantheon and its founding records. Nothing here adds gods or doctrine — it is play, in the open, with what the founder has recorded.

Game One — Mix the Gods

Choose as many gods as you like and mix them. The table will show you who they are together: their kinship, the houses and generations they span, and the work their combined masteries would govern.

Game Two — Ask the Gods

Type any subject, and the gods whose domains touch it will be seated to share their knowledge — composed on the spot from their recorded roles, their functions to the Heart, and (where the founder’s simulations have reached them) their recorded wisdom.

The gods speak from the founding records. Their answers are the record, arranged — the page invents nothing.

Nu Miracles of ROM — founded by Hoc, 2026. Nu means “new” — and “And You”: you are part of this story.

The founding record is kept by the founder. Stories are drafts awaiting the founder’s judgment; forums, debates, and simulations are the gods’ voices at play, not settled doctrine — each page says which is which.
