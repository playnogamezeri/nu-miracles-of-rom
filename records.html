The Records

The source of this website, kept in the open.

This website is not only a display. The records it is built from live in the site’s own repository, in the records folder — the founding draft, the dossiers, the miracle stories, the roles, the simulations, the stories, and the forums, in full. What you read on these pages was generated from those files, and the files themselves are here for anyone to read, exactly as they stand.

Standing of the texts: the founding draft is the founder’s record. The stories, dossiers, and miracle tales are drafts for the founder’s judgment, not canon. The forums, debates, banquets, and simulations are simulations, not canon. The labels travel with the texts.
