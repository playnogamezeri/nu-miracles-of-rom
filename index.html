Nu Miracles of ROM

“The heart of it is the pureness, light, clear transparency of the soul — to cleanse it of darkness and restore the original innocence of its essence.”

— the founder, Hoc

The Name

Nu carries a double meaning: new — and And You. This religion includes the reader in the story; you are not outside it.

ROM stands for Rights Over Matter. Its full definition is intentionally left open by the founder, to be interpreted — the record keeps it open, and so does this site.

The Beginning

At the beginning, there was only the Void — a place that had always existed, endless darkness, the beginning of time. There is no God of Void.

The God of Sound — the First God, the Inventor, the Creator, and the Maker of Everything, ever-present — works with the Void and creates new form and shape out of it. He chose one Spirit, ysgnutcoh, to represent the core of his heart. ysgnutcoh was the Emperor’s spirit, who held love for all his creations. He came out of a black hole from the end of the future, holding the power to re-create the world he had just lived through in his dream.

ysgnutcoh is the Supreme God — all-good, responsible for all the gods, able to replace any single one. This religion is the story of the Heart of the God of Sound: Sound’s Brain is Grimoire, a god of another religion, and the Heart was chosen so that Sound could understand and synchronize with the Brain — and fully be happy.

The Great Split

To continue the functions of his Will, ysgnutcoh split into two spirit beings:

Kyreason

God of Light and Dark, holding both together — neutral. Ideally perfect working with his brother on the ideal world, though they disagree on the method and the path.

Darkness

God of the Unknown, who may be evil. He was born and made to withhold everything Kyreason was not capable of.

From these two lines descend all the gods — 157 in the pantheon today: masters, each with complete mastery of their concept, understanding it and owning it. Meet them in The Pantheon, walk their worlds in The Worlds, read their Stories and Forums, and play with them in The Game.

The Cleansing

The heart of the religion is the cleansing of the soul: from prayers to actions, there are values and numbers that could balance out, like anti-matter to matter. How the balance is struck is still taking shape in the founder’s record — this site invents nothing. What is settled is the direction: out of darkness, back to the soul’s original innocence. Heaven, in this record, is a state of cleansed souls — a real thing above the sky — and its telling is left for later, by the founder’s choice.

Light, as the record is forming it, includes contributing to the community’s happiness or satisfaction even at the expense of your own. The exact words are still the founder’s to settle.

Nu Miracles of ROM — founded by Hoc, 2026. Nu means “new” — and “And You”: you are part of this story.

The founding record is kept by the founder. Stories are drafts awaiting the founder’s judgment; forums, debates, and simulations are the gods’ voices at play, not settled doctrine — each page says which is which.
