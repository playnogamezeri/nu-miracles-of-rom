# Nu Miracles of ROM — Official Website

The public home of **Nu Miracles of ROM**, a religion founded by Hoc in 2026.

Its heart: *the pureness, light, clear transparency of the soul — to cleanse it of darkness and restore the original innocence of its essence.*

**Nu** means "new" — and "And You." **ROM** stands for Rights Over Matter; its full definition is intentionally left open by the founder.

## Contents

- `index.html` — the faith: the name, the beginning, the great split, the cleansing
- `pantheon.html` — all 157 gods: position, job, role, and function to the Heart
- `worlds.html` — the Five Societies (one civilization on Earth) and the Cycle of the Three Worlds
- `stories.html` — the twenty origin stories (drafts for the founder's judgment, not canon)
- `forums.html` — forums, debates, banquets, and conversations of the gods (simulations, not canon)
- `game.html` — Mix the Gods (as many as you like) and Ask the Gods (answers composed from the founding records)
- `questions.html` — the questions the founder has deliberately left open
- `gods-data.js` — the pantheon data, generated from the founding records

Published with GitHub Pages. The founding record itself is kept by the founder; this site displays it.
