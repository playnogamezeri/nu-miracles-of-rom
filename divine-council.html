The Divine Council

Bring a question before the Gods. Choose the masteries that should be represented, then examine what their recorded domains contribute.

The Council is a structured reflection tool, not a claim of literal supernatural communication. It uses the existing pantheon and does not create Gods, assign allegiances, or declare new canon.

1. Present your question

Suggestions are ranked by textual overlap with each God’s recorded name, job, role, function, group, lineage, and wisdom. You can always choose different members.

2. Choose the Gods at the table

Select up to 12 existing Gods. The council begins with your selections; “Suggest a council” can replace them with the strongest matches to your question.

0 of 12 seats selected

How to read a council result

  • Mastery: the God’s recorded job.
  • Role and function: the pantheon’s current descriptions of that God’s place and purpose.
  • Recorded wisdom: shown only when an entry exists; it may be draft or simulated, not settled doctrine.
  • Collective reflection: a reasoning framework for the question, not a fabricated quotation or an official divine verdict.

For a more open-ended topic exploration, visit Ask the Gods. For personal calculations and planning, visit the Divine Toolbox.
