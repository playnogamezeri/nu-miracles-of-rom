The Divine Council

Bring a question before the Gods. Choose the masteries that should be represented, then examine what their recorded domains contribute.

The Council uses a transparent deliberation engine: it compares recorded masteries, shows the passages that match your question, identifies recurring language and possible trade-offs, and builds a provisional reasoning process. It is not a claim of literal supernatural communication.

1. Present your question

Suggestions use weighted textual relevance and try to represent different pantheon groups. You can always choose different members. Relevance is not a measure of divine authority.

2. Choose the Gods at the table

Select up to 12 existing Gods. The engine will compare their recorded masteries, roles, functions, and wisdom against your question. It will show its evidence and distinguish textual overlap from actual agreement.

How the deliberation engine works

  • Mastery: the God’s recorded job.
  • Role and function: the pantheon’s current descriptions of that God’s place and purpose.
  • Recorded wisdom: shown only when an entry exists; it may be draft or simulated, not settled doctrine.
  • Shared language: recurring terms in multiple records, not proof that the Gods agree.
  • Possible tensions: concepts that may need balancing, not evidence that the Gods disagree.
  • Provisional record: what the source supports, what remains uncertain, and what needs the founder’s decision.

The engine is deterministic and runs in your browser. It does not call an AI service or invent divine dialogue. For a more open-ended topic exploration, visit Ask the Gods. For personal calculations and planning, visit the Divine Toolbox.
