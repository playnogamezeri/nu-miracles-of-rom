The Divine Toolbox

Six divine systems for cycles, decisions, relationships, resources, development, and harmony.

This is a divine-first symbolic system. Its equations organize the pantheon into repeatable readings; they do not claim guaranteed knowledge of future events.

Choose a tool below. Each one draws on the names, roles, and domains in gods-data.js, so the toolbox grows naturally as the pantheon grows.

01 · God of Time · God of Destiny · God of Forecast

Birthday Forecast

A personal daily reading for today and tomorrow, using your birthday as a symbolic starting point.

Your birthday stays in this browser page; it is not sent to a server. This is a symbolic divine reading, not a guarantee of future events.
